01 Who we are

Creative thinking.
Precise execution.

C Flow is an event planning studio built around one idea: your celebration should look remarkable and still feel like you.

We combine creative direction with practical planning, trusted supplier coordination and hands-on event management. The result is a cohesive experience where every detail serves the bigger picture.

Whether it's an intimate proposal or a large wedding celebration, we tailor the process to the event rather than forcing the event into a package.

The founder

Charbel Habchy

Founder & Creative Director

C Flow was founded to give clients one trusted point of contact for the creative and operational sides of an event. Charbel leads each project with a focus on communication, thoughtful design and making the planning experience as smooth as the celebration itself.

His approach is collaborative: understand the couple or client first, then build the event around their priorities, personality and budget.
